    How it works

    1. You choose a dish, portion size, extra oil or butter and the number of servings.
    2. A RandomForest model trained on typical nutrition values predicts the protein, carbs and fat for your meal.
    3. Calories are calculated as 4 x protein + 4 x carbs + 9 x fat.
